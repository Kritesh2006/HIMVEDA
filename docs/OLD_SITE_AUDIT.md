## Technical problems fixed

- Canonical and `og:url` tags pointed at `himveda.theripplebytes.com`, telling search engines to rank another domain.
- Prices were US-dollar-sized numbers prefixed "Rs." (e.g. "Rs. 14.00").
- Privacy and Terms read "Full policy text to be finalised with legal counsel". FAQ answers were empty. The contact page had no contact details.
- A bundle listed two items but charged for one. A video file named after another brand ("aura-wellness-promo.mp4") was left in.
